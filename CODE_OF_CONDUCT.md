# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a place where people stick to the issue and treat each other with respect. This code is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Address the issue, not the person: discuss views and evidence, and don't attack whoever raised them.
- Respect different schools and opinions. When you disagree, bring sources and comparisons, not volume.
- Be patient with newcomers; no question is too simple.

## What we don't accept

- Personal attacks, insults, harassment or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing other people's private information without their consent, including phone numbers, addresses and their birth data.
- Using chart readings to intimidate or disparage a specific person.
- Spam and promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that violates this code, and may lock discussions or stop an account from participating when necessary. To report a problem, @-mention a maintainer in an issue, or use the contact details in [`README.zh-CN.md`](./README.zh-CN.md) §十「关于与联系」.
